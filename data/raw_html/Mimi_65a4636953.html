Battle[]

It can summon much stronger enemies like the Proto-behemoth, Xolotl, and the Mánagarmr. It is also temporarily invincible when summoning. It will only summon once per battle, and after summoning it will only cast Ruin. It is important to note any Bamapama that are summoning along with Mimi.

The summoned monster depends on how many Bamapama are helping the summoning Mimi. The more Bamapama, the stronger the monster. Mánagarmr is summoned when is one or no Bamapama remaining on the field; two or three, a Xolotl is summoned; four or more, a Proto-behemoth.

Since Bamapama aren't invincible while summoning, it is recommended to attack them before the Mimi can summon a Proto-behemoth.

Etymology[]

[view · edit · purge]Mimis are fairy-like beings of Arnhem Land in the folklore of the Indigenous Australians of northern Australia that live in a different dimension from humans. They have thin and elongated bodies, and to avoid breaking in high wind they spend most of their time living in rock crevices. They are said to have taught the Aborigines of Australia how to hunt, prepare kangaroo meat and use fire.

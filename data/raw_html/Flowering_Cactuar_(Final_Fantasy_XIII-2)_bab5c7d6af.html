The Flowering Cactuar is an enemy in Final Fantasy XIII-2. As soon as the battle begins, the cactuar will begin slinging elemental spells at the party. When captured, its name changes to Cactrot.

Paradigm Pack[]

The Flowering Cactuar is a Medic. It has a ridiculously high Strength compared to the Magic stat, and the two can only be leveled up with Mana materials. It is a very good Medic early-mid game, but because of its low HP stat it could quickly fall out of favor late-game. It must also learn Esuna via infusion. However, it has a quick casting animation, paired with Improved Cure II, and its Feral Link may inflict Curse on targets, which is a debilitation neither Serah nor Noel can possess.

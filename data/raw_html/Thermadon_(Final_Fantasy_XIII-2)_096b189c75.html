The Thermadon is an enemy in Final Fantasy XIII-2, found in some of the floors of Augusta Tower in 200 AF. It is a shell-covered Armadon-type enemy that needs to be staggered to deal good damage against. It is weak to elements.

Paradigm Pack[]

Thermadon is a Commando with a low development cost and high stats, with resistances to physical and magic spells. Once maxed out, it will have Strength in excess of 1,000 and low Magic.

It has a red-locked passive Leadenstrike, which halves the ATB-recharge rate. A good way to overcome it is to infuse a Fencer level 18 and a Yeoman level 1 to get Attack:ATB Charge II and Siphon Boost II, which override Leadenstrike.

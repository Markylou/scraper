Jihl Nabaat is a downloadable content boss from Final Fantasy XIII-2. She is among the various lost souls bound to battle in the Coliseum until the Day of Reckoning in the trial known as "A Study in Elegant Death".

Battle[]

People's hearts are pure chaos. Formless and ever-changing—a mystery no one can solve. That is why humans are such contradictions. One moment they're at each other's throats, the next they're forming shaky alliances. Will this happen to you two, I wonder. I'll hold out my hand, and smile while I give you my terms. Now die!

Jihl to Serah, Mog and Noel

Nabaat summons reinforcements. With the exception of the Azure Behemoth, the reinforcements are not a significant threat in terms of damage (although they can be more problematic by virtue of numbers), and are more of an annoyance; for instance, by inflicting Poison, or launching party members into the air.

Nabaat destroys her minions with the Sacrifice Pawn technique and then absorbs them. It is used after all the minions are killed or a certain amount of time has passed. She will always Sneer beforehand. Absorbed Nightblade Pumas cause the party to take damage and be Dazed for ten seconds, and each PSICOM Warden heals her for 99,999 HP. If all Azure Behemoths are dead before Sacrifice Pawn, Nabaat will take damage equal to one quarter of her maximum HP. She cannot kill herself with this backfire, as it can only lower her HP to 10% of her maximum HP.

Nabaat uses her special attack, Sadistic Surge, after every Sacrifice Pawn. It is strong enough to kill whoever it hits. If she absorbs two Azure Behemoths, she will use the more powerful Malicious Surge instead which also cures her of status ailments. Both attacks dispel her buffs. If not careful, it can wipe out both Noel and Serah. Afterwards Nabaat summons a fresh set of allies.

At the start of the battle, Nabaat summons two PSICOM Wardens, two Nightblade Pumas and an Azure Behemoth, and uses Sacrifice Pawn after 20 seconds. After this, she summons a total of four PSICOM Wardens and Nightblade Pumas in any combination and an Azure Behemoth, and sacrifices them after 50 seconds. Below 40% HP, she summons two Azure Behemoths, one of which may be replaced by a total of four PSICOM Wardens and Nightblade Pumas like before, and sacrifices them after 35 seconds.

Nabaat has a high stagger point and chain resistance, so staggering her is not easy. She casts buffs such as Protect and Shell (with Defensive Maneuvers) and Bravery (with Offensive Maneuvers) on herself and her allies, which in combination with her halving both physical and magical damage, makes damaging her more difficult. Nabaat is susceptible to most debuffs, although it can take many attempts to get them to stick.

Strategy[]

Given that Nabaat destroys her own minions with the Sacrifice Pawn technique, a well-prepared party could just focus on attacking Nabaat. However, this is not generally an advisable strategy, as the three enemy types confer some benefit to her when she absorbs them; consequently, the priority should be to eliminate all PSICOM Wardens before she sacrifices them.

As she takes damage trying to absorb an already-defeated Azure Behemoth, this can be an effective way to deplete her HP if the player can kill the summoned enemies fast enough to prevent any from being absorbed.

If the player includes a Saboteur in their paradigm deck, Poison and Imperil can be useful, and Dispel is also beneficial.

The player can change paradigms to one with at least one Sentinel to reduce damage from Sadistic Surge.

Paradigm Pack[]

Nabaat is a useful Saboteur, whose attacks complement Serah and Noel's limited skillsets. She is quick with both physical and magical movements, helped by her good Strength and Magic. Her HP is average.

Nabaat's Feral Link, Sadistic Surge, is strengthened with a control stick input and magically hits one foe multiple times. The attack is best used during an enemy's stagger period, as it does minimal damage otherwise.

Nabaat and Dendrobium are the only Paradigm Pack allies with access to Poisonga; all others have only the Strength-based, physical-damage Heavy Poisonga. She is the only Saboteur with both Improved Debuffing and Improved Debilitation II. Thus, she is the most adept at reducing both the effectiveness of both enemy defenses (Debuffing) and abilities (Debilitation). All of her Debilitation spells are of the physical-damage "Heavy" or "II" type.

Nabaat has a unique purpose other Saboteurs cannot replicate: by Cursing her opponent and then steadily attacking it, Nabaat can catch a target in a stun-lock via her Pressure II passive ability. Her downside is that Nabaat will attempt to inflict most of the debuffs she knows on a target before moving on to Wound, which has the best Cut (hitstun value). This strategy is ineffective against many bosses and Coliseum opponents.

All-Mana build is good for her to capitalize on the Wound damage and to strengthen her Magic-based Feral Link, but using Potent materials is viable as well. Due to her unique role as a stun-locker, Nabaat becomes less effective the more spells one teaches her.

Nabaat's Feral Link, Sadistic Surge, is powerful, good at keeping the opponent stun-locked, and bestows additional bonuses to Nabaat depending on the Paradigm Pack's role composition:

  • 1 Synergist: Bravery
  • 2 Synergists: Bravery and Faith
  • 1 Sentinel: Protect
  • 2 Sentinels: Protect and Shell
  • Commandos: Damage Boost
  • Ravagers: Chain Boost
  • Medics: Heals Nabaat, Small Damage Boost
  • Saboteurs: Increased Wound Damage

Behind the scenes[]

Nabaat uses the Sanctum's Antimatter Manipulation Principle technology to create artificial magic and call reinforcements.

Jihl Nabaat is the only deceased character from Final Fantasy XIII whom the player can recruit into their Paradigm Pack.

In the Extra Episode, Snow's Story: Perpetual Battlefield, all Coliseum participants appear to fight against Snow and Lightning, including Jihl Nabaat, along with PSICOM soldiers who praise her.

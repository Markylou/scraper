Triffid
The Triffid is an enemy in Final Fantasy XIII-2. It appears with other Triffids or with Goblins in the sunny Archylte Steppe. It is a plant monster that is not very powerful, has low defenses, and low HP, and so should not be difficult to defeat.

Behind the scenes[]

Triffid is mentioned in the Brain Blast quiz, which reveals that it was among the most popular monster figurines at -0 AF-. "Triffid" is also an answer to one of the possible questions in Captain Cryptic's Confounding Quiz: "What looks like a flower but stings like a bee?"

Etymology[]

[view · edit · purge]Triffid is the name of a fictional venomous plant in John Wyndham's novel The Day of the Triffids.

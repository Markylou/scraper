The Buccaboo Ace is an enemy in Final Fantasy XIII-2. It leads groups of Buccaboos, bestowing enhancements that aid them in combat. If it is destroyed first, a Buccaboo will use Rise to Power in order to turn itself into an Ace, so the player may wish to defeat the accompanying Buccaboos first.

Paradigm Pack[]

Buccaboo Ace is a potentially useful tool and its role and passive abilities are almost all good enough to keep, or infuse to other monsters; leveling up an Ace will unequivocally yield some returns. Its Feral Link Spirit Infusion heals all team members and casts on them Faith, Protect, and Shell.

Battle[]

The universe stretches into infinity! What life can survive its expanse? Dare you face the envoy of the stars?

Arbiter of Time introducing the PuPu as battle starts.

While the UFO is active, it heals active PuPus and deploys new ones to replace the fallen. PuPus have basic physical attacks, either a single attack or a three-hit one, both doing the same total damage. After every 30 seconds, they cast fire or thunder spells at the same time. Both spells hit twice, and can be devastating due to PuPus' high magic

When the UFO is staggered, it falls helplessly to the ground and becomes a close-range target, and all PuPus cease attacking and run around the coliseum in a panic.

When the UFO is defeated, a total of ten PuPus will evacuate from it before it crashes into the battlefield, inflicting heavy damage to the party. Afterwards, the PuPus will use Rain down, wayward stars! and start using Meteor nine seconds after that. Meteor calls down a meteorite onto every party member immediately and after every 15 seconds afterwards, doing massive, defense-piercing, magical damage in a small radius, causing wound damage and launching. This can easily dispatch an unprepared party.

Pupu drops CP, gil, items and its monster crystal only once, no matter how many are actually defeated during the battle.

Strategy[]

See also: UFO (Final Fantasy XIII-2)

Using the Cerberus paradigm can take out PuPus relatively quickly while also damaging the UFO. When PuPus gain the ability to cast Meteor, Protection (SEN/MED/SYN) and Combat Clinic (SEN/MED/MED) can help mitigate damage. With an array of status enhancements and exceptionally high HP, the player should go with Aggression (COM/COM/RAV) as Cerberus (COM/COM/COM). It might not be able to cut down the initial number of PuPus, but slightly charged chain gauge can help in quicker dispatch. Later, Cerberus can be used to deal with a smaller group.

Paradigm Pack[]

PuPu is a reasonably good Medic. By making a Paradigm Pack consisting of Fantasy or Champion monsters, PuPu can use its Pack Mentality and gain an additional 30% boost to its Magic. While it learns all of the major Medic spells, its true strength lies as a monster recruiter. With a good timing on the player's part, the Synchronization gauge can easily reach close to 999%; PuPu's Feral Link is Items Please!, which inflicts magic damage and increases the odds of obtaining items after battle. The player should get the opponent's health low enough, switch to the Salvation paradigm, and use Items Please! for maximum effectiveness. If the player fails to defeat the target, they can wait while PuPu uses Cheer to fill its Feral Link gauge.

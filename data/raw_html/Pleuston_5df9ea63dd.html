Paradigm Pack[]

Once tamed, the Pleuston becomes a Ravager. It is a Well-Grown monster surpassed by a similar enemy, the Nekton. At level 10, the Pleuston learns Overwhelm, an ability useful for infusion.

Etymology[]

[view · edit · purge]Pleuston is "a buoyant mat of weeds, algae, and associated organisms that floats on or near the surface of a lake, river, or other body of fresh water."

Paradigm Pack[]

Meonekton is a Commando whose development is slightly weighted towards Strength. Its stat development is largely linear, but its stats at its maximum level are low, even for an Early Peaker. It can inflict Deprotect with its Feral Link, which makes Meonekton slightly useful in the very early stages of the game. If one is not caught before defeating Atlas in Bresha Ruins 005 AF, a Gate Seal must be used to obtain one.

Etymology[]

[view · edit · purge]A nekton is a subcategory of ocean-dwelling creatures capable of free movement independent of water currents, as opposed to plankton, which cannot. This includes vertebrates such as fish, molluscs such as squid, and crustaceans such as crabs or lobsters. Like all enemies of the Rift Beast class, the name is derived from a real class of living organism that also form part of an ancient clade of the evolutionary tree, referring to their origins in the Void Beyond.

Meonekton's Japanese name is "Meiobenthos". [view · edit · purge]Meiobenthos, also called meiofauna, are small benthic invertebrates that live in both marine and fresh water environments.

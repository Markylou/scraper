Battle[]

Unlike most other creatures of the Bomb type, who bear a specific elemental weakness, Debris is not weak to any elements. Instead, using non-elemental magical attacks, such as Ruin, stops them from growing and eventually self-destructing.

Strategy[]

The player should use a paradigm with multiple Commandos, such as Cerberus (COM/COM/COM) or Aggression (COM/COM/RAV).

Paradigm Pack[]

Debris, which can be found near the place where Cloudburst spawns, is considered one of the best Bomb-type monster

Etymology[]

Debris is rubble, wreckage, ruins, litter and discarded garbage/refuse/trash, scattered remains of something destroyed, discarded, or as in geology, large rock fragments left by a melting glacier etc. Depending on context, debris can refer to a number of different things.

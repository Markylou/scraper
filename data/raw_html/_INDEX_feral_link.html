Feral Link (シンクロ・ドライブ, Shinkuro Doraibu?, lit. Synchro Drive) is a gameplay element in Final Fantasy XIII-2. It allows the player to execute a recruited enemy's special attack by correctly performing the necessary actions required by a triggered Cinematic Action.

Trophy: Wild Thing
Successfully used Feral Link abilities a significant number of times.

Successfully using Feral Link abilities 100 times earns the "Wild Thing" achievement/trophy.

Mechanics[]

Each monster has its own Feral Link gauge, which gradually fills over time in battle while not KO'd. Some Feral Link abilities take longer to charge than others, depending on the monster. The gauge will continue to fill even when the monster is not in the active paradigm, and its progress will carry over after battles or when removed from the Paradigm Pack layout. The rate at which the gauge fills can be increased with the Feral Speed passive ability, with the highest ranked version on each party member stacking cumulatively. A monster's gauge will also fill when they are healed; the increase is proportional to the amount healed compared to their max HP.

Once a monster's Feral Link gauge fully charges, the border around their portrait will turn orange in battle and green in the menu. Pressing / executes the monster's special ability. Afterwards, controller inputs determine the "Synchronized" percentage. The higher the synchronization percentile, the higher the chance that an enemy defeated by the Feral Link will be tamed, up to a maximum of 300%.

The meanings of each input type are as follows:

  • Single button: The player is required to press the button shown on-screen or move the left analog stick in the desired direction. The faster this is done, the higher the Synchronization percentage.
  • Multiple: The player must make the inputs in the order shown on the screen, from left to right, as quickly as possible. The faster this is done, the higher the synchronization percentage. However, a wrong input will reduce the percentage considerably.
  • Timing: Three button icons converge. The player must press the button the moment the icons have fully converged. Pressing the button too early or too late results in a reduced synchronization percentage.
  • Button tap: The player must repeatedly tap the button shown until the outer ring is completely orange.
  • Stick rotation: Starting from the top, the player must rotate the left analog stick in the direction shown until the outer ring is completely orange.
  • Complex: The cinematic action uses more than one of the above input types.
  • None: No input is required.

While performing a Feral Link, a distortion similar to one seen in Final Fantasy XIII during a full ATB skill execution appears, albeit at a different angle. Many Feral Links will slow battles down for a brief period, and the battle time runs at half speed over the duration of one. Chain gauges will stop depleting, and enemies will not be staggered until it ends. Both the party leader and enemies will not be able to act until the Feral Link ends, unless they have already initiated a series of actions.

List of Feral Links[]

Feral Link abilities have varying effects, from dealing damage and inflicting status ailments, to healing the party and bestowing status enhancements. Some status effects can only be applied by the party via Feral Links, including Daze (Typhon), Defaith and Debrave (Tezcatlipoca), Regen (Bloodfang Bass, Breshan Bass, Ceratoraptor, Pitterpatter, Spiceacilian), Reraise (Cactuarama) and Slow (Don Tonberry, Nekton, Samovira, and Ultros).

Other appearances[]

Pictlogica Final Fantasy[]

Feral Link is the Premium Skill for Serah. When activated in Force Memento, it deals 4000 physical damage to one enemy. When activated in Wisdom Memento, it deals five hits of 500 magic damage to one enemy. When activated in Prayer Memento, it restores 2000 HP and cures weakening debuffs to the party. When activated in Defense Memento, it increases the party's Defense and Magic Defense for 2 turns. It has a slow charge speed.

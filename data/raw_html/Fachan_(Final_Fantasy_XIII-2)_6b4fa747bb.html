Paradigm Pack[]

Once it has been tamed, the Fachan becomes a Synergist. It learns four commands, and its passive skillset lacks rare abilities. Its low stat growth makes it limited in use, and its two notable abilities, Enfrost and Enaero, are not learned until late in its development.

Etymology[]

[view · edit · purge]The Fachen is a creature in Scottish and Scots-Irish folklore. Its appearance is said to be so frightening that it induces heart attacks.

This enemy's Japanese name is "Direach", a mythological creature and type of Fachen.

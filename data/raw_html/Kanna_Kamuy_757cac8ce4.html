Battle[]

It will attack with a breath ability that inflicts Daze, Poison, and Deprotect on one target, and then use Feeding Stoop on that same target, inflicting a great amount of additional damage.

Strategy[]

The party should take him down as quickly as possible in order to prevent him from using this devastating combo.

Paradigm Pack[]

The Kanna Kamuy is a Saboteur and its skillset is based around dealing physical-based damage. It is capable of inflicting Curse, which neither Serah nor Noel are capable of. Its stat development is geared towards maximum HP, but none of its stats are high.

Etymology[]

[view · edit · purge]Kanna was a Japanese era name after Eikan and before Eien. This period spanned the years from April 985 through April 987. The reigning emperors were En'yu-tennō (円融天皇) and Ichijō-tennō (一条天皇).

[view · edit · purge]Kamuy, often rendered as Kamui, is the word for a spiritual or divine being in Ainu mythology. Kanna Kamuy is one such being, that of thunder and lightning, and is said to have accompanied Kamuy-huci, the goddess of the hearth, as she descended from the heavens.

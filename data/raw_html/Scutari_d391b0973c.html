The Scutari is an enemy in Final Fantasy XIII-2. It can be found with other Scutari or with a Testudo.

While it is simple to defeat when encountered alone, when the Testudo uses Issue Orders, all Scutari will use Version Upgrade, granting them a number of beneficial status effects. These effects will make the Scutari more irksome, but they should still not be a major threat to the party.

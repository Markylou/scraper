Battle[]

When not staggered, Navidon is protected by its carapace. It uses ice attacks and can restore its own HP, and can wound the party.

Strategy[]

It is recommended that a Navidon only be challenged after executing a preemptive strike to allow an easy first stagger, and to ensure its defenses will drop to a point where Serah and Noel can deal a significant amount of damage to it. After the first stagger ends, the party can use Relentless Assault or Cerberus to start refilling the chain gauge, then switch to Tri-disaster.

Paradigm Pack[]

The Navidon, like the Pulse Gladiator, is a skilled Sentinel, mainly due to its unparalleled resistances. It lacks high HP, having half as much as the Bunkerbeast, but it receives half the total amount of damage or less from all types of enemy attacks and is far easier to heal.

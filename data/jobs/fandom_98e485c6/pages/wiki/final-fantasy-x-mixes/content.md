## Guide

Items in boldface can be purchased from a shop . Others must be obtained through different means.

Not every shop sells every item: Grenade and Map are not available until the Mi'ihen Highroad ; Distiller items are not available until the Via Purifico ; Holy Water is not available until the Calm Lands ; and Al Bhed Potion is exclusively from Rin onboard the Fahrenheit after gaining control of it. Although Clear Sphere is obtained from the Monster Arena shop, unlocking it requires capturing five of every monster.
